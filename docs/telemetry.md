# Nodely Telemetry

[Nodely](https://nodely.io) runs a free telemetry service for Algorand nodes. Once it's on, your node sends health and performance data to Nodely, and you get a dashboard of it on their site. It's handy for keeping an eye on your node from your phone, or when you're away from your computer.

## Turn it on

1. Make sure your node is running.
2. Open the [Manage menu](/manage) and choose **Enable Telemetry**.
3. Choose **Open Nodely Telemetry** from the same menu to see your node's dashboard on Nodely.

To stop sending data, choose **Disable Telemetry**.
